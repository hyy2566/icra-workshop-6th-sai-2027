# Visual assets

## Original hero artwork and typography

`hero-interaction-v2.webp` is used as the homepage hero and social preview at the organizer's request. It was generated with the built-in image generation tool; the original prompt remains in `HERO_PROMPT.txt`. It is conceptual artwork, not a photograph of a particular research result. Authentic previous-workshop photographs remain in the gallery.

Manrope font files are served locally from `fonts/`. Source: Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope). License: `fonts/OFL.txt` (SIL Open Font License).
